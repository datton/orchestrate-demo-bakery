Picking up

Straight to your order, never to the back of the line.

    Watch your number

    Your order page and the screen in store show when it moves from being made to ready.

    At Old Town, ready orders wait on the shelf inside the door, sorted by number. At Riverside, ask at the counter.

    Show your code

    Show the QR code or say your 4-digit pickup code, and it is yours. A friend can collect it with your pickup link.
