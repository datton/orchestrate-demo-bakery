About us

Slow dough, warm hands.

Hearth & Crumb is a bakery with two doors and one habit: we start before dawn so you don’t have to wait.

Baked at dawnSourdoughButter croissantOrder aheadCollect warm

We fold our croissant dough over three days, feed a sourdough starter that is twelve years old, and let loaves rise overnight. The only thing we rush is the trip from the oven to your bag.

The idea

Why order ahead

The best loaves and the last almond croissants go early.

So you can order from your phone, pick a bakery, and have your bake set aside with a pickup number. At the counter we see the order you built, so what you chose is what is waiting.

It is a demo shop, so payments run in test mode and no real money moves.

How we work

Three habits

One

Time, not shortcuts

Long fermentation and patient folding give flavour that no additive can.

Two

Baked every morning

The counter is filled fresh at dawn. Best eaten the day it is baked, especially the baguettes.

Three

Honest about allergens

Our kitchen handles wheat, dairy, nuts and eggs. If you have an allergy, ask at the counter before you order.
