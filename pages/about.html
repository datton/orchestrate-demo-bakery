About Hearth & Crumb Bakery

Slow bread, flaky pastry, good coffee.

Hearth & Crumb is a neighbourhood bakery with two doors, one oven mindset and a habit of letting dough take the time it needs.

We would rather bake one loaf properly than ten quickly. Ordering ahead lets us do both: you skip the wait, and the bread gets all the time it needs.

What we care about

Small things, done every day.

Long ferments

Our country sourdough rests for 30 hours, which is where the blistered crust and the tangy, open crumb come from.

Real butter

Croissants take three days from dough to oven, with cultured butter folded into 27 layers.

Nothing wasted

Yesterday's croissants become tomorrow's almond croissants, filled with frangipane and baked again.

Cakes for occasions

Slice by slice at the counter, or a whole cake ordered a day ahead for a later pickup.

About this site
