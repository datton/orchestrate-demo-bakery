Good bread takes time and good mornings do not have any to spare. So we do the slow part overnight, and you do the quick part: order ahead, walk in, and pick up something still warm.

How it works

Four taps. Warm bread.

Pick 1 / 4

  1. Choose your bakery and fill your bag.

    Loaves, pastries, slices and coffee, with every option priced as you tap. Whole cakes can be ordered ahead for a later pickup.

  2. Pay in a few seconds, with the best code already applied.

    Gift cards from your wallet come off first. This is a demo shop, so payments are test payments only.

  3. Watch it go from received to being made to ready, live.

    Your pickup number, how many orders are ahead of you and when it should be ready, right on your phone.

  4. Walk in, find your number and show your code.

    Running late? Share the pickup link and a friend can collect it for you.
