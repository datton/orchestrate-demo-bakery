The best part of a coffee shop is the coffee, not the queue. So we made the queue optional: order from the ferry, the sidewalk or your desk, and walk straight to the shelf where your name is waiting.

How it works

Four taps. Zero waiting.

Pick 1 / 4

  1. Choose your store and build your drink exactly how you like it.

    Size, milk, shots, sweetness, syrups: every option shows its price as you tap, and you can save it as your usual.

  2. Pay in a few seconds, with the best code already applied.

    Gift cards from your wallet come off first. This is a demo shop, so payments are test payments only.

  3. Watch it go from received to being made to ready, live.

    Your pickup number, how many orders are ahead of you and when it should be ready, right on your phone.

  4. Walk past the line to the pickup shelf and show your code.

    Running late? Share the pickup link and a friend can collect it for you.
