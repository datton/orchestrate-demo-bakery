About

We are a small bakery with a simple idea: make good bread and pastries by hand, from simple ingredients, and share them with the people who live nearby.

Our bakers start before sunrise. Dough is mixed, shaped and left to rest, so that the loaves are ready when the doors open. Pastries are rolled and folded by hand, and cakes are baked in small batches.

This is a demonstration bakery, so the details on this page are examples, not a real shop.
